PRIVACY NOTICE

A simple explanation of what this site stores and what happens when you open an external form.

AI Ready Colombo does not use its own analytics tracker or advertising cookies. Course progress and reflection text are saved locally in your browser; assessment and feedback forms open separately on Google Forms.

Direct answer

You can browse the learning pages without creating an account. The website itself keeps module progress and reflection notes on your device using browser local storage. Personal information is only submitted when you choose to use an external form that asks for it.

ON THIS WEBSITE

What the site stores locally

Module progress

When you mark a module complete, the site saves that status in your browser's local storage. It is used only to show your progress on that device.

Reflection text

Text entered into reflection boxes is stored locally in your browser so it can still be there when you return. The website code does not send those reflections to a server.

Cookies and analytics

AI Ready Colombo's own code does not currently set analytics cookies, run advertising trackers or use a site analytics service.

Clearing local data

You can remove locally stored progress and reflections by clearing this site's storage in your browser. Doing so resets the saved information on that device.

EXTERNAL FORMS

Assessments and feedback are hosted separately.

Baseline assessment

The baseline opens in Google Forms. Information you submit there is sent to that form rather than stored by the static website.

Final assessment and certificate

The final assessment collects the information needed to score the quiz and, when eligible, issue a certificate. The certificate workflow uses Google Sheets, Apps Script, Slides, Drive and Gmail to create and send the certificate.

Feedback

The published feedback form is voluntary and does not ask for a name or email in its question set. Avoid putting identifying or sensitive information into optional text answers unless you want it included in your response.

Third-party privacy

When you open Google Forms, LinkedIn, GitHub or another external site, that service's own privacy terms and technical processing apply.

HOSTING & DELIVERY

Services used to deliver the site

GitHub Pages

The website is designed to be hosted on GitHub Pages. As the hosting provider, GitHub may process ordinary technical request data needed to deliver the site.

jsDelivr

The site loads selected animation libraries from the jsDelivr content delivery network. A request to that network is made when those scripts load.

No advertising

The site does not contain third-party advertising and AI Ready Colombo does not sell participant information through the website.

Retention

Assessment and certificate records are kept for pilot administration, reporting and certificate records. No automatic deletion schedule is currently configured for the pilot.

Keep sensitive information out of forms and prompts unless it is genuinely necessary. For the learner-facing guide to AI data controls and safer sharing, see Your Data & AI.
