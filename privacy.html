YOUR DATA & AI

Before you paste something into AI, know what you're sharing.

History, memory, personalisation and model improvement are different controls. Turning one off doesn't automatically turn the others off.

Four things people often mix up

01

History

Whether past conversations stay visible in your account.

02

Memory

Whether the assistant can remember information for future conversations.

03

Model improvement

Whether eligible interactions can be used to improve future models.

04

Feedback

What can happen when you deliberately submit a rating or feedback.

CHECK THE TOOL YOU USE

Provider settings change. These instructions are a learner-friendly summary and should be rechecked against the providers' official privacy/help pages before each new cohort.

PAUSE BEFORE YOU PASTE

Some information should make you stop and think.

Passwords & codesBank/card detailsPassport/NIC numbersPrivate client dataConfidential work filesMedical recordsPrivate information about someone elseRestricted exam material

Rule of thumb: if you'd be uncomfortable accidentally emailing it outside your organisation, don't casually paste it into a consumer AI tool.

TRY IT NOW

Open the AI tool you use most.

  1. Find its data or privacy settings.
  2. Check whether your conversations may be used for model improvement.
  3. Check whether chat history or activity is being saved.
  4. Look for memory, personalisation, temporary chat or incognito options.
