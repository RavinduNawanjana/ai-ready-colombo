Responsible AI

Question outputs, protect your data and keep humans in the loop.

Course progress 0%

01 / LEARN

Four reasons to slow down before you trust an output.

H

Hallucinations

AI can invent facts, sources or details.

B

Bias

Outputs can reflect skewed data or assumptions.

P

Privacy

Not everything belongs in a consumer chatbot.

D

Dependency

If AI does all the thinking, you may stop noticing when it's wrong.

02 / VERIFY

Before you use an important AI answer, ask:

  • Can I verify the main claim independently?
  • Is there an original source I can open?
  • Is the information current?
  • Could missing context or bias change the answer?
  • Am I sharing anything sensitive?
  • Should a human make the final call?
