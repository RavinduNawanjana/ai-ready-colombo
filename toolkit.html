QUICK PROMPTS

Useful starting points. Not magic words.

Good prompts come from clear thinking. Copy these, change them and make them fit the task in front of you.

01

Universal CTCO

Context: [your situation]
Task: [what you want done]
Constraints: [limits, tone, audience, rules]
Output: [the format you want]
02

Learn something

Teach me [topic] at a beginner level. Use one simple analogy, then test me with five questions one at a time. If I get one wrong, explain why before continuing.
03

Research better

Help me scope research on [topic]. First identify the five key questions I need to answer. Then suggest the kinds of credible sources I should look for. Do not invent citations.
04

Critique writing

Review the draft below. Do not rewrite it yet. Identify the three biggest weaknesses in clarity, structure and evidence. Suggest one concrete fix for each.
05

Interview practice

Act as an interviewer for an entry-level [role]. Ask eight behavioural questions one at a time. After each answer, give me one strength and one improvement.
06

Challenge my thinking

I'm considering [idea/decision]. Don't simply agree. Identify my assumptions, give me the strongest counterargument, and list what evidence would change the conclusion.

VERIFY BEFORE YOU USE

A quick check before an AI answer becomes your answer.

  • Open the original source when possible.
  • Check dates — old information can still sound current.
  • Verify important numbers and quotes independently.
  • Ask what context could be missing.
  • Keep sensitive information out of prompts unless it is appropriate to share.
  • Make the final judgment yourself when the stakes are high.
