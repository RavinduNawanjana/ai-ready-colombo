AI DISCLOSURE

AI helped build parts of this project. It did not replace the person responsible for it.

Generative AI was used as a development tool for drafting, editing, coding, design exploration and quality checks. Ravindu Nawanjana reviewed, selected, revised and approved the published project.

Direct answer

No generative AI model runs inside the AI Ready Colombo website, and the site does not send learner reflections or form responses to a generative AI service. AI was used during development, while the final content, structure and release decisions remained under human review.

WHERE AI HELPED

Development assistance

Writing and editing

AI assisted with early drafts, rewrites, summaries and copy refinement. Published wording was reviewed and edited before release.

Code and interaction design

AI assisted with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, animation ideas, debugging and implementation checks for the static website and supporting automation.

Learning design

AI was used to explore examples, exercises, checklists and ways to organise the five-module learning journey and toolkit.

Quality checks

AI assisted with consistency checks, link and layout review, accessibility considerations and identifying areas that needed manual verification.

WHAT STAYS HUMAN

AI does not get the final say.

Published content

Ravindu Nawanjana is responsible for the final selection, editing and approval of the material published as AI Ready Colombo.

Participant assessment

The final assessment uses predefined quiz questions and a fixed pass mark. Certificate automation follows those rules; a generative AI model is not grading participants or deciding who receives a certificate.

Learner reflections

Reflection boxes are stored locally in the learner's browser. They are not automatically analysed by a generative AI model.

External platform instructions

AI product settings and policies can change. Platform-specific guidance should be checked against the provider's current official documentation before relying on it.

LIMITS

What this disclosure does not promise

AI can still be wrong

AI-assisted drafting can introduce errors, omissions or stale information. The project encourages verification for exactly that reason.

Educational, not professional advice

The course is practical AI literacy material. It is not a substitute for legal, medical, financial or other professional advice.

Transparency is part of the lesson. The project uses AI where it is useful, discloses that use, and keeps responsibility for important decisions with people.
