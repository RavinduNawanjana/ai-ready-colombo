ABOUT THE PILOT

A small learning product with a simple question: can practical AI literacy make people better users of AI?

AI Ready Colombo is designed as a short pilot for young people navigating study, early careers and an increasingly AI-enabled workplace.

What the pilot is trying to do

01

Make AI less mysterious

Explain the useful parts without turning the course into a technical lecture.

02

Build practical habits

Prompt clearly, verify what matters, protect sensitive information and keep human judgment in the loop.

03

Measure something

Use a baseline, final assessment and learner feedback instead of relying on impressions alone.

04

Stay small enough to finish

Build, pilot, learn, then decide whether expansion is worth it.

Project attribution

AI Ready Colombo is a project by Global Shapers Colombo Hub, designed as a small, measurable pilot in practical AI literacy.

PROJECT LEAD

Developed by Ravindu Nawanjana.

Ravindu developed AI Ready Colombo as a practical AI literacy pilot for the Global Shapers Colombo Hub. He shaped the learning structure, toolkit, website and pilot workflow around one goal: helping learners use AI more effectively without handing over judgment.

The project is intentionally small and testable. The first version combines five short modules, a baseline and final assessment, a downloadable toolkit and participant feedback so the next version can be informed by real use.
